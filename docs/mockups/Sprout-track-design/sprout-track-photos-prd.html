Photos — UI PRD

Product: Sprout Track (PWA)  ·  Feature: Photo capture, attachment & gallery  ·  Version: 1.1 Draft  ·  Date: Jul 13, 2026  ·  Owner: John Overton

Prototype: sprout-track-photos.html (clickable, mobile-first)

1. Overview

Caregivers capture hundreds of baby photos but Sprout Track has no way to keep them alongside the care record. This feature adds photos as a first-class citizen: a dedicated Photo activity on the Log Entry page, the ability to attach photos to existing activities (Feed and Milestone in v1), thumbnails on the timeline, and a Photos gallery page in the main navigation for browsing by month, milestone, or all.

Design principle: photos always live in context. Every photo is anchored to a moment on the timeline — either its own Photo entry or an activity it is attached to — and the gallery is a second lens over the same data, never a separate silo.

2. Goals & Non-Goals

Goals

  • Log a photo in under 10 seconds from the Log Entry screen.
  • Attach up to 4 photos to Feed and Milestone activities at log time or later from the record view.
  • Surface photos on the timeline as thumbnails without crowding activity rows.
  • Browse, search, favorite, and bulk-manage all photos from a dedicated gallery page.
  • Mobile-first: every surface works one-handed on a phone (PWA), and scales up to desktop.

Non-Goals (v1)

  • Photo editing (crop, rotate, filters).
  • Attachments beyond Feed and Milestone ship after v1 — agreed rollout adds Bath, Activity, and Measurements next (Sleep, Diaper, Pump, Medicine, Vaccines not planned).
  • Sharing/exporting albums outside the family account.
  • Video.

3. User Stories

  • As a parent, I snap a cute moment and log it as a Photo with a caption and optional milestone tag, so it appears in today's timeline.
  • As a parent logging a feeding, I attach the "first avocado" photos to the feed entry so the memory stays with the data.
  • As a caregiver reviewing the day, I see photo thumbnails inline on the timeline and tap one to view it.
  • As a parent, I open the Photos page and browse by month or by milestone, search captions, and favorite the best shots.
  • As a parent, I select several photos and delete or download them in one action.

4.2 Photo activity (Log Entry)

  • A Photo button joins the activity icon row (picture icon; final illustration TBD — ASSET NEEDED).
  • Opens the Photo Tracker drawer (right-side sheet on desktop, full-screen on mobile) with two tabs, mirroring the Vaccine Tracker pattern:
    • Add Photo: upload dropzone (camera/library/drag-drop), taken date & time (defaults to now, editable — EXIF date pre-fills when available), caption, milestone tag (select or create).
    • Photo Library: search bar + list grouped by taken date; each row shows thumbnail, caption, time · type · milestone, favorite toggle, and opens the photo detail. Link out to the full gallery.
  • Saving creates a Photo entry in today's timeline and adds the photo to the gallery.
  • A storage-quota meter sits under the drawer tabs so caregivers see usage at the point of upload.

4.3 Timeline thumbnails

  • Any activity with photos shows thumbnails right-aligned before the timestamp (44 px, rounded).
  • Thumbnail count adapts to available width — 3 on desktop, 2 on mobile — with a +N overflow badge when more exist.
  • Tap a thumbnail → photo detail. Tap the row → the activity's record (Feed Record / Photo Record).
  • Daily Summary gains a "Photos Today" stat that deep-links to the gallery.

4.4 Attaching photos to activities (Feed + Milestone, v1)

  • The Log Feeding drawer gains a Photos section: attached thumbnails with remove (×), and an add tile. Limit 4 per activity TUNABLE.
  • The Feed Record detail shows a "Photos (N)" section; photos can be viewed or added after the fact. Empty state: "No photos attached to this feeding yet."
  • Milestone entries use the same attachment model; a photo tagged with a milestone at upload links automatically.
  • Roadmap: the same attach pattern extends to Bath, Activity, and Measurement entries next.

4.5 Photo records & detail

  • Photo Record (timeline entry with 1–4 photos): time, caption, caretaker, milestone, photo grid; Delete / Edit / Close footer, consistent with other record drawers.
  • Photo detail (single photo): large preview with favorite overlay, inline-editable caption, taken date/time, type chip, milestone chip, linked activity, and favorite / download / delete actions.
  • Deleting a photo moves it to Trash (§4.6) and hides it everywhere it appears; deleting a record removes the timeline entry but leaves its photos in the library.

4.6 Trash (30-day recovery)

  • Deleting a photo anywhere (detail drawer, lightbox, bulk select) soft-deletes it to Trash; timeline thumbnails, records, library, and gallery hide it immediately.
  • Trash is reached from the gallery toolbar (with item count). Each item shows a days-remaining badge on its tile; items can be selected singly or in bulk for Restore or Delete Forever.
  • Auto-purge runs server-side 30 days after deletion; purging also removes activity links.

5. Mobile / PWA Requirements

  • Bottom tab bar replaces the sidebar under 940 px; drawers become full-screen sheets; lightbox stacks vertically.
  • All tap targets ≥ 44 px; icon row scrolls horizontally with no scrollbar chrome.
  • Capture path uses <input type="file" accept="image/*" capture> so "Add photo" offers camera or library natively.
  • Uploads queue offline and sync when connectivity returns (service-worker background sync) ENG.
  • Client-side downscale/compress before upload (target ≤ ~1 MB per photo); originals optionally retained per family setting OPEN.
  • Storage quota is per family and configurable in the Family Manager settings page (mention only — not mocked); usage % surfaces in the gallery header and the Photo Tracker drawer.

7. States & Edge Cases

  • Empty gallery: friendly zero-state with an Add Photos CTA.
  • No search/filter results: "No photos match" with hint to clear filters.
  • Upload failure: tile shows retry state; entry saves without the photo rather than blocking the log.
  • Permission denied (camera/library): inline explainer with a link to settings.
  • Large libraries: month groups virtualize/lazy-load images; thumbnails only in grid, full asset on lightbox open.
  • Nursery Mode: photo capture available; gallery management hidden OPEN.

8. Success Metrics

  • ≥ 40% of weekly-active families log at least one photo in week 1 post-launch.
  • Median time from Photo button tap to saved entry < 10 s.
  • ≥ 15% of Feed entries carry an attached photo by week 4.
  • Gallery page ranks in top-4 visited nav destinations within a month.

9. Decisions (Jul 13, 2026 review)

  1. Storage quota: per family, configurable in the Family Manager settings page. Usage % shown in the gallery header and Photo Tracker drawer.
  2. Favorites: per caregiver.
  3. Delete semantics: soft-delete to Trash with 30-day auto-purge, plus Restore and Delete Forever.
  4. Milestone auto-suggest: not in scope.
  5. Attachment surfaces: Feeds, Bath, Activity, Milestones, Measurements.
Handoff note: all flows above are demonstrated in the clickable prototype (sprout-track-photos.html): Photo Tracker drawer (both tabs), feed attach, Feed/Photo Records, photo detail, gallery grouping/search/filters, lightbox, and bulk select. Placeholder gradient tiles stand in for real images.
