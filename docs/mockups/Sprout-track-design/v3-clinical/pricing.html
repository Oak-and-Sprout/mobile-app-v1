Questions parents actually ask

What happens after the 14 days?

We remind you before the trial ends. If you don’t subscribe, your account pauses; nothing is deleted, and you can export your data or subscribe later.

Do grandparents or the nanny cost extra?

No. Unlimited caretakers are included in every plan. Invite the whole care team.

Which plan should I pick?

If you’ll track for more than seven months, Lifetime is cheaper. If you’re not sure yet, start monthly; you can switch anytime.

Is my baby’s data private?

Yes. No ads, no trackers, nothing sold. The entire codebase is public on GitHub, so you can verify that instead of trusting it.

Can I leave and take my data?

Anytime. Export your full history with one click, and import it into a self-hosted instance if you like.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes: it runs in the browser on any phone, tablet, or computer. No app store, nothing to install.
