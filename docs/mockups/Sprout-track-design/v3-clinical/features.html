Logging

Two taps, thumb-sized targets

Forms lead with your usual amounts and both hands stay optional. Timers handle naps, nursing, and pumping sessions; everything else is a couple of taps.

  • Last amounts pre-filled
  • Works in any browser, phone to laptop
The care team

Shared with everyone who helps

One family timeline for parents, grandparents, and the nanny. A link and a PIN gets someone in; every entry is signed with who logged it.

  • Unlimited caretakers on every plan
  • Multiple babies, one-tap switching
Health records

Vaccines, medicine, and growth

Record each vaccine dose with the provider and attach the clinic’s paperwork. Track medicine schedules and chart weight, height, and head circumference over time.

  • Images and PDFs stored with each record
  • Provider contact book
Nursery mode

The crib-side tablet display

Dimmed, always on, readable across a dark room. Big tiles log a feed or start sleep in one tap. The screen never locks mid-change.

  • Dim, hue, and saturation sliders
  • Choose which tiles appear
