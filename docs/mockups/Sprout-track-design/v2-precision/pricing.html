FAQ

Straight answers

What happens when the trial ends?

You get a reminder before day 14. If you don’t subscribe, the account pauses. Nothing is deleted; export your data or subscribe whenever.

Is $2.99 really the whole price?

Yes. No per-caretaker seats, no premium tier, no feature unlocks. One family, one price.

Can I move from hosted to self-hosted later?

Yes. Export from the hosted service and import into your own instance. It’s the same codebase.

What exactly does Lifetime cover?

Hosting, every feature, and all future updates for one $19.99 payment. If you’ll track longer than seven months, it’s the better deal.

What do you do with my data?

Store it, back it up, and serve it to your family. No ads, no analytics resale, no third-party trackers. The code is public, so this is verifiable rather than a promise.

What does self-hosting require?

Any machine that runs Docker: a Raspberry Pi, a NAS, a $5 VPS. Clone the repo, run docker compose, open port 3000.
