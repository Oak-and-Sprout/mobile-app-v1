Fair questions

What happens when the trial ends?

You’ll get a heads-up before day 14. If you don’t subscribe, your account pauses; nothing is deleted, and you can export your data or pick a plan whenever you’re ready.

Do grandparents and nannies cost extra?

No. One plan covers your whole family: unlimited caretakers and unlimited babies, always.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes, always. Export your full history anytime. And because Sprout Track is open source, you can move from our hosting to your own server and keep everything.

What does Lifetime actually include?

Everything, forever: hosting, all features, and all future updates, for one $19.99 payment. If you track more than seven months, it’s the cheaper option.

How is the hosted version different from self-hosting?

The software is identical. With hosting we run the server, keep backups, and apply updates, so it just works from any device. Self-hosting is the same app on your own machine, free.

Is my baby’s data private?

Yes. No ads, no trackers, no selling data. The code is public on GitHub, so you don’t have to take our word for it.
